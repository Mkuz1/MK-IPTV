How it works

Watching in three steps

  1. Install MK IPTV

    On any of your screens. The demo playlist opens straight away, so you can look around first.

  2. Add your playlist

    Enter the server address, username and password from your IPTV provider. Your library loads in seconds and stays cached.

  3. Link your other screens

    Sign in with Apple or Google, or type an 8 character code on the TV. Everything appears there too.

Privacy

Your logins stay yours

Encrypted on the device

Playlist passwords live in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore, never in a plain file.

Sealed before they sync

With sync on, passwords are encrypted end to end on your device first. The cloud only ever stores ciphertext.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Does MK IPTV include channels or films?

No. MK IPTV is a player. It plays the subscription you already have from your IPTV provider, using the Xtream Codes login they gave you.

What do I need to get started?

Your provider's server address, username and password. Most providers send these when you subscribe. Until then the built in demo playlist lets you try every feature.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Apple TV from the App Store, Android phones, tablets and Google TV as an app download. A native Mac version is in App Store review.

Do I need an account to sync?

No. You can sign in with Apple or Google, or skip accounts and link devices with a one time 8 character code. Sync is off until you turn it on.

Is my provider password safe?

It is stored in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore. When you sync, it is encrypted on your device before upload, so the cloud copy can't be read.

Can I watch without internet?

Yes, for anything you download. Films and whole seasons download in the background and play offline. Live TV needs a connection.

Can I watch on my TV from my phone?

Yes. Cast to Chromecast and Google TV, or use AirPlay to an Apple TV or AirPlay TV, straight from the player.

Why won't some files play on iPhone?

Apple's player can't open MKV and AVI files. Those titles play in the Android and Google TV apps, or you can cast them to a TV.
